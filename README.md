# AKPsi Website Demo

Build a modern, premium, fully responsive multi-page marketing website for Alpha Kappa Psi — Beta Upsilon Chapter at the University of South Carolina. AKPSI is USC's premier co-ed professional business fraternity.

Reference for STRUCTURE & CONTENT ORGANIZATION ONLY (do not copy its visual style — it looks dated): https://uscsou.com — use it to understand how to organize sections, brothers, pledge classes, and alumni. The visual design must follow the direction below, which is far more polished.

Brand & Design System


Colors: Deep navy #0A1F44 (primary), metallic gold #C8A24B (accent), warm cream/off-white #F7F4EC (light backgrounds), near-black #0B0E14 (cinematic sections). White text on navy, navy text on cream. Use gold sparingly as an accent — thin rule lines, button borders, small labels, underlines. Never make gold the dominant fill.
Typography: Editorial and confident. Display headings in Fraunces or Playfair Display (serif, prestigious). Body and UI in Inter. Small all-caps gold labels (letter-spaced) above section headings.
Aesthetic: Clean, premium, lots of whitespace, full-bleed photography, generous type scale, subtle fade/slide-in-on-scroll animations. Think "prestige consulting firm meets flagship university" — NOT a templated frat site. Rounded-corner cards with soft shadows, smooth hover states, a sticky nav that gets a subtle navy background on scroll.
Global polish: Add a favicon (a gold AKPSI monogram on navy). Ensure it's a clean HTTPS build. Mobile-first responsive at every breakpoint.


Global Nav (sticky, top)

Logo/monogram on the left. Links: Home · About · Brothers · Recruitment · Alumni · Contact. On the right, a standout gold pill CTA button: "Rush Fall 2026" that links to the Recruitment page. Mobile: hamburger → full-screen navy overlay menu.

Global Footer

Navy background. AKPSI Beta Upsilon wordmark, the tagline, quick nav links, social icons (Instagram, LinkedIn — placeholder links), and a small line: "© 2026 Alpha Kappa Psi, Beta Upsilon Chapter · University of South Carolina." Include a "Powered by AKPSI Productions" credit line.


PAGE 1 — Home


Hero: Full-bleed background photo (use an AI-generated / stock image of well-dressed college students in business-professional attire on a collegiate campus). Navy gradient overlay for text legibility. Headline: "Alpha Kappa Psi · Beta Upsilon". Subhead: "USC's premier, co-ed, professional fraternity creating opportunities to develop professionalism, foster leadership, build networks, and enhance abilities." Two buttons: gold "Rush Fall 2026" + outlined "Learn More."
Award badge band: A bold strip: "Voted the #1 Professional Business Fraternity at the University of South Carolina — 18 consecutive years." Make it feel like a trophy/seal moment with a gold accent.
Stats counters: Animated count-up tiles (placeholder numbers, clearly easy to edit): e.g., "150+ Active Brothers," "18 Years #1," "90%+ Internship Placement," "500+ Alumni Network." Label each.
"Where We're Working" logo wall: A clean grayscale-on-hover logo grid of employers. Pillars preview: Short 5-card row teasing the values (links to About → Pillars).

PAGE 2 — About (with sub-sections, can be one long page with anchor nav or tabs)

Pillars: Five cards for AKPSI's core values — Brotherhood, Knowledge, Integrity, Service, Unity — each with a short description, an icon, and a placeholder photo of brothers embodying it. (Flag in a code comment to verify the exact five values.)

FAQ: Accordion of general questions about the fraternity (what AKPSI is, who can join, time commitment, business vs. all majors, GPA, dues — placeholder answers). One FAQ item about rush should link to the Recruitment page.

PAGE 4 — Recruitment  ⭐ (THE HOLLYWOOD "NOW CASTING" PAGE — make this one cinematic)

This page breaks from the navy/gold corporate look into a Hollywood movie-premiere theme while staying on-brand.


Hero: Near-black cinematic background with a vintage cinema marquee vibe (use a stock/AI image of a lit theater marquee at night, OR leave a hero slot for an uploaded marquee graphic). Big marquee-style headline: "NOW CASTING: Alpha Kappa Psi — Fall 2026 Rush." Add subtle marquee-light bulbs along borders, a red-carpet accent, and gold/warm-white glow. Tagline copy: "AKPSI is going full Hollywood. Bring your best outfits, lots of popcorn, and your friends when we roll out the red carpet this semester."
Why Rush?: Benefits of the pledging process. Include 2–3 short testimonial cards (placeholder) from a recent pledge, the VPR, and the Director of Recruitment.
Rush Schedule: A timeline/filmstrip of rush events. Use placeholder dates/times/locations clearly marked "Dates TBA — check back soon," styled like clapperboard or ticket cards (Info Night, Meet the Brothers, Professional Night, Social Night, Interviews, Bid Day). Make these trivially easy to edit later.
Dress Guide: Business casual & professional guidance for men, women, and gender-neutral options, with placeholder photos of brothers in each.
Recruitment FAQ: Accordion. Include placeholder recruitment contacts (two names + emails), general rush questions, and at least one question with light tips for the case competition (keep it general).
Big closing CTAs: Two gold buttons — "Apply for Fall 2026 Rush" linking to https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfCgCYZZ8K5KUr3uVBS8XOXzayMvuNbqIZ1XZAC8yRIBwwDmA/viewform and "Get Rush Text Updates" linking to https://www.remind.com/join/akpsi2025. Use film motifs throughout: filmstrip dividers, clapperboard icons, ticket-stub cards, a red-carpet section. Keep it tasteful and premium, not cheesy.


PAGE 5 — Alumni

Alumni Testimonials: Card grid answering "How did AKPSI prepare you?", "Favorite AKPSI memory?", "Advice for incoming recruits?" (placeholder quotes + names + grad years).

Crone Scholarship Donation: A clean donation section with a placeholder "Donate" button and short copy about supporting the chapter's educational fund.


PAGE 6 — Contact


A clean split layout: a working contact form on one side (Name, Email, Subject, Message — wire it to a simple form handler / mailto placeholder), and a contact directory on the other listing President, VPF, VPE, VPR, and Director of Alumni Relations (placeholder names + emails). Include the chapter's general email and Instagram. Add a small "University of South Carolina · Columbia, SC" line.


Technical notes

Smooth scroll, scroll-triggered fade/slide animations, accessible color contrast, keyboard-navigable.
